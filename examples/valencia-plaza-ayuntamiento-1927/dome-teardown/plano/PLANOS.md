# Película fotorrealista de Correos — 7 planos (estilo del clip de los canteros)

Orden final: mapa que se despliega (mío) → plano 1 → 2 → 3 → canteros (ya hecho) → 4 → 5 → 6 → cúpula interactiva.

Bloque de estilo (se pega al final de cada prompt):
  Hyper-realistic cinematic shot, 19th-century Valencia, Baroque and late-Gothic Valencian
  houses in brick, plaster and carved stone, terracotta tile roofs, wooden pole scaffolding
  lashed with rope. NO columns, NO neoclassical architecture. Warm low golden-hour sunlight
  from the left, long soft shadows, dust floating in the sunbeams, weathered skin and
  fabric, 35mm film, shallow depth of field, 4k. Workers in period clothes: striped linen
  trousers, red sash, alpargatas, headscarf or beret.
Negativo: columns, pillars, neoclassical, modern buildings, cars, 3D render, CGI, plastic skin, text, watermark, distorted faces, flat lighting

1. Vista cenital, excavación: A top-down aerial view of an excavation pit in a city block, workers digging by hand, mule carts carrying earth away. Slow descent.
2. Vista de pájaro, estructura: Aerial three-quarter view of a six-storey stone building rising inside timber scaffolding, wooden derricks lifting stone blocks on hemp ropes. Slow orbit.
3. Descenso a la calle: The camera descends from above the scaffolding to eye level in a stone yard; stonecutters at benches, a mule cart.
4. Esquina andamiada: Low angle, the camera tilts up a scaffolded stone corner while a derrick lifts a stone block on rope; masons on the planks.
5. Travelling por la fachada: The camera dollies sideways along a stone facade with arched windows and iron balconies, past a hand winch with a turning drum and workers.
6. Contrapicado a la torre: Dramatic low angle looking up at a stone building with a slender iron lattice tower and glass dome behind, clouds drifting, scaffolding being removed.
